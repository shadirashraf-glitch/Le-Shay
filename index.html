What is needed to reconcile

  • Once: the manager's cash on the first trading day, and the date each advance was handed over.
  • Daily: cash sales and UPI or card sales as separate figures.
  • Daily: how much spend was paid from the till, and how much by UPI or bank.
  • When it happens: cash deposited in the bank or handed back to the owner.
  • Weekly: a physical count of the cash on hand.
