Post-opening funding

Three different kinds of money, kept separate.

Operational cash advances

Working capital given to run the cafe day to day. Not an expense: it pays for costs already in the daily ledger.

Owner-paid October operating costs

Paid directly by the owner, outside the daily ledger. Counted once, in October's result.

Owner-Funded Stock

Bought with the owner's personal funds and supplied to Le Shay. Not from manager cash, not in daily expenses, and not counted as an immediate cost.

Stock purchases by funding source

Separates stock the owner bought directly from stock bought with the manager's operating cash. Owner-funded stock counts towards owner funding but is not treated as an immediate expense. Manager-cash stock comes from the daily ledger and is already in recorded spend.

What is needed to reconcile

Four extra lines on each daily page, plus a one-off opening figure, are enough to calculate the expected cash and compare it with a physical count.

  • Once: the cash the manager had on the first trading day, and the dates each advance was handed over.
  • Daily: cash sales and UPI or card sales, as separate figures.
  • Daily: how much of the spend was paid from the till, and how much by UPI or bank.
  • When it happens: cash deposited in the bank, or handed back to the owner.
  • Weekly: a physical count of the cash on hand, to check against the expected figure.
